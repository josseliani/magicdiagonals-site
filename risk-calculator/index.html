Important for accurate sizing

Risk = price distance × contract size × lots × USD conversion. The calculator assumes a linear price-to-profit relationship. Forex crosses quoted outside USD need conversion into the account currency. It does not include spreads, commissions, slippage, margin limits or open trade exposure. The daily result must be entered by you; it is not read from MT5.
