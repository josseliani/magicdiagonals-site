Get started in 5 minutes

  1. Open any chart on TradingView → Indicators → Invite-only scripts. Add RCC Pro or RSI Retest Scalper Pro. If you don't see them yet, refresh the page.
  2. In the indicator settings, the first line is Preset. Pick the preset for your instrument and set the chart to the exact symbol and timeframe in its name.
  3. Leave the other settings unchanged until you understand the statistics table. Judge a preset by the target row in its name (1R or 2R).
  4. Read the guide for your indicator below.
  5. Create alerts on the instruments and timeframes you trade. After changing a preset, symbol or timeframe, create the alert again.
